About NooriTools

Welcome to NooriTools — a collection of smart, simple and useful online tools designed to make everyday digital tasks easier.

What is NooriTools?

NooriTools brings useful tools together in one convenient website. Instead of searching for a different website for every small task, users can access practical tools from one place.

Our Goal

Our goal is to create simple, fast and easy-to-use tools that anyone can understand and use without complicated software or installations.

Our Tools

NooriTools includes tools for calculations, currency conversion, dates, percentages, units, text, passwords, QR codes and other everyday tasks.

Free and Easy to Use

We aim to keep NooriTools accessible and convenient. Our tools are designed with simplicity, usability and responsive design in mind, so they can work across computers, tablets and mobile devices.

Our Commitment

We continuously work to improve NooriTools, fix problems and add useful features. We also aim to provide clear information and a trustworthy experience for our visitors.
